User Data Deletion Instructions for Duck Bot

Duck Bot is an automated educational application that posts videos about migratory wildlife. We do not store or collect your personal data on external servers.

According to Facebook Platform rules, users have the right to request deletion of any associated data. Follow the steps below to manage or remove Duck Bot permissions:

How to Remove Duck Bot from Your Facebook Account:

  1. Go to your Facebook Profile's Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Scroll down and click on Security > Apps and Websites (or Business Integrations).
  3. Find Duck Bot in the list.
  4. Click the Remove button next to Duck Bot.
  5. Check the box to delete all posts or interactions if prompted, and click Remove.

Manual Deletion Request:

If you wish to submit a formal request for confirmation that no personal records remain, please email us directly with the subject line "Data Deletion Request":
