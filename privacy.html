Privacy Policy for Duck Bot

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Duck Bot ("we", "our", or "the App") operates in connection with Facebook Page management and automated educational content creation regarding migratory birds and wildlife conservation.

1. Information We Collect

Duck Bot is an automated content publisher. We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal identifiable information (PII) from general Facebook users or visitors.

  • Page Management Data: The application only utilizes API tokens provided by the Facebook Page administrator to publish educational wildlife videos and reels directly to the designated Facebook Page.
  • Analytics & Metrics: We may review aggregate, non-personal page insights (such as video views and reach) provided by Facebook to optimize educational content reach.

2. Use of Information

Any technical data or access tokens used by Duck Bot are strictly used to:

  • Publish daily automated wildlife reels and informational posts about migratory bird species along the Indus Flyway in Pakistan.
  • Maintain automated scheduling and content delivery.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not share any personal user data with third parties. Content is fetched from public wildlife repositories (such as Pixabay) adhering to their standard API terms.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Duck Bot does not store end-user personal data on external databases, there is no end-user data retained. If you are an administrator and wish to revoke access, you can remove Duck Bot permissions anytime via your Facebook Business Integrations settings or contact us.
